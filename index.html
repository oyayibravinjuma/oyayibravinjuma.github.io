01 / ABOUT

A journey shaped by resilience.

Oyayi Bravin Juma is a Kenyan university student, aspiring author, spoken-word artist, public-speaking enthusiast, youth leader and emerging innovator. He comes from the Kachibora area of Trans Nzoia County, Kenya, around the Cherang'any Hills.

His outlook has been shaped by resilience, Christian faith and a belief in God and in his own capacity to keep learning and growing. Childhood health challenges and setbacks in his education taught him to value hope, persistence and the support of faith.

Bravin is interested in using communication, education, creativity and technology to make a positive contribution. He is particularly passionate about encouraging young people and people who have lost confidence in their future to regain direction and pursue meaningful goals.

02 / THE JOURNEY

Every chapter can
teach you something.

EARLY YEARS

Roots in Trans Nzoia

Bravin traces his roots to Kachibora, in the Cherang'any Hills area of Trans Nzoia County. Experiences in childhood, including periods of illness, helped shape his faith and appreciation for life.

SECONDARY SCHOOL

A second attempt

He attended Kamusinde Boys High School and later repeated Form Four at Terem SA Secondary School, a sub-county school, to sit the KCSE examination for a second time. The experience strengthened his commitment to perseverance.

DECEMBER 2025

Spoken-word recognition

He won a general spoken-word competition at the Waks na Kazi Concert in Bungoma County, hosted by Senator Hon. Wakoli.

2026

University chapter

He joined Kibabii University in 2026 and is pursuing studies in Mathematics and Biology.
